## Outcome

## Read set

## Steps

1. Use local approved sound IDs with bounded active voices. Crossfade habitat beds and preserve unrelated layers.
2. Keep occasional events on their own timing/choice channel; avoid one global loop restart on every cell crossing.
3. Expose safe gain/fade/timing settings and report missing sounds rather than downloading replacements.

- An actual listening route checks fades, localisation where relevant and repetition; headless PASS alone does not finish the task.
