## Outcome

The project has evidence about its real terrain/precision limits before downstream systems depend on a coordinate strategy.

## Read set

Read the root rules/orientation/recent log, then the metadata read set and actual files being changed. Read dependency evidence when its result fixes a version or interface. Do not read every task.

## Allowed changes

Only the metadata paths, plus this task's evidence report, its relevant feature walkthrough, CODE_GUIDE.md, its PLAN.md row and one appended DLOG.md row. Directory scopes are not permission for unlimited changes. Do not change rules/contracts unless explicitly listed.

## Steps

1. Test a controlled scene near zero, at +/-100 km and +/-1000 km, plus repeated crossings of the proposed recenter boundary. These are test distances, not claims of universal support.
2. Compare the same logical sample, nearby contact/movement and horizon before and after a candidate recenter/backend handoff. Do not merely move the camera to zero.
3. If a viable strategy fits the narrow probe, implement only that probe path. Record graph offsets, collider/viewer treatment and precision limitations. Otherwise supply a failed-feasibility report.

## Acceptance

- Retain exact poses, sample comparisons with justified tolerances, screenshots/video and collision observations.
- A failed probe may be accepted as an investigation, but must explicitly block backend acceptance. No changed seed or shifted landscape is passed as recentering.
- Run `python tools/project.py validate` and the existing helper tests. Explain the changed code in ordinary language and update its feature walkthrough.

## Non-goals

No full terrain rewrite, custom engine compilation or untested "infinite" claim.

## Stop conditions

A strategy requires changing generation semantics or exceeds the small prototype scope. Also apply AGENTS.md: stop after two failed focused repairs, for unapproved spend, unavailable required evidence, or when the coordinator must split the scope.
