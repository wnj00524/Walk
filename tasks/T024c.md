## Read set

Read the root rules and recent log, then the art-direction, asset-registry,
and visual-capture walkthroughs and the actual approved GLBs/fixture.

## Allowed changes

Only local approved-asset material binding/provenance and the labelled import
inspection fixture, plus this task's evidence report, relevant walkthrough,
CODE_GUIDE.md, PLAN.md row, and one appended DLOG.md row. No new paid jobs,
terrain, placement, renderer, save, or camera-route changes.

## Outcome

The three approved representative GLBs render with their intended local PBR
materials in the isolated asset fixture and the integrated fixed-seed route.

## Acceptance

- Each approved asset has a verifiable material with a non-null albedo texture
  and the intended local texture provenance.
- The asset fixture reports textured materials and retains one real mesh per
  candidate without Blender dependency.
- The integrated vegetation suite and fixed PNG capture pass without fallback
  geometry or missing-asset diagnostics.

## Steps

1. Inspect current material/texture bindings and provenance.
2. Repair using existing local derivatives or approved local asset bytes only.
3. Run the fixture, asset, vegetation, and visual checks and retain evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop for missing local texture bytes, required paid generation, or any scope
larger than material binding/provenance repair. Apply AGENTS.md's two-repair rule.

## Non-goals

No new asset generation, terrain, placement, renderer, save, or camera-route
changes.

## Close the session

Report result, changed files, actual checks, accessible evidence,
documentation, unverified items, and the next task. Do not start the next task.
