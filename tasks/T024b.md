## Outcome

Each vegetation batch is positioned at its signed logical cell origin while
records remain local to that cell. Nearby streamed cells therefore render in
their true world locations without changing placement ownership or save data.

## Acceptance

- A batch for cell `(x, z)` has world origin `(x * 256, 0, z * 256)` metres.
- Records retain local half-open coordinates and are not double-translated.
- The nine-resident-batch bound and stale/release lifecycle remain intact.
- The vegetation suite and fixed real-renderer capture pass, with readable
  fixed-seed grouping retained under `artifacts/T024a_visual_capture`.
