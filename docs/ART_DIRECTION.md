# Art direction and owner review

## First landscape palette

A believable temperate mountain/forest setting: woodland, meadow, exposed rocky upland and later small lakes. This is an initial proposal. Begin with one fixed daylight condition, restrained colour, readable ground detail and recognisable distant landforms. No buildings, ruins, roads, fantastical plants, artificial monuments or simulated animals.

## What procedural beauty means here

Large shapes establish ridges, slopes, valleys and basins. Habitats respond to those shapes rather than scatter everything uniformly. Trees form irregular groups and gaps. Rocks belong to consistent families. Quiet stretches alternate with openings and longer views. Do not create a new spectacle every few steps or change the landscape behind the walker to force a reveal.

One authored review fixture is allowed for proving assets/materials. It must be labelled as a fixture. It is not evidence that procedural seeds look good. The later visual gate requires several fixed generated seeds, approach/return routes and an unselected random-seed sample recorded before capture.

## Review viewpoints

Use identical camera poses, seed, world version, quality setting and light for comparisons. Capture: an open view towards a ridge, a forest interior at eye height, a ground/roots/rocks close-up, a habitat edge and a return to the starting view. After water exists, add a shore and an elevated basin view. Capture motion too; still images can hide popping and bad movement.

## Acceptance rubric

For every view, mark ACCEPT, REVISE or NOT REVIEWED with one sentence of evidence.

| Area | Accept when | Typical revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landforms | The place reads as a connected landscape | Reduce uniform noisy bumps; define stronger regional structure |
| Ground contact | Plants and rocks sit naturally on visible ground | Fix height/normal placement, not cosmetic hiding |
| Materials | Roughness, scale and colour belong together | Correct the outlier material/asset family |
| Vegetation | Silhouettes and grouping look believable at eye height | Reject malformed assets; change clustering and scale ranges |
| Distance | Distant landmarks remain the same when approached | Fix backend/detail-level consistency |
| Motion | No distracting popping, shimmer, sway or walking jitter | Record a reproducible route and isolate the cause |
| Sound | Layers are natural, restrained and locally appropriate | Change one layer's gain, timing or sample |

The owner decides whether the experience is enjoyable. Agents translate feedback into explicit changes. Do not certify beauty using an invented numerical score or a headless test. Do not keep adding asset variants when the lighting or placement rules are the real defect.

## Deliberate deferrals

Day/night cycles, dynamic weather, seasons, global climate bands, rivers with regional drainage, caves, wildlife and photogrammetric-scale asset libraries are outside the first acceptance scope. Water begins with intentionally generated basins; rivers require a separate regional-drainage design rather than painting a flat strip through random terrain.
